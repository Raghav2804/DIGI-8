CareerPulse — Job Market Analysis and Recommendation System

Better career decisions start with better signals.

CareerPulse turns job listing data into practical market insights. It helps job seekers understand demand, compare salary information, and find roles aligned with their skills and preferences.

Problem statement

Job seekers often have to piece together fragmented information about hiring demand, compensation, work arrangements, and skill requirements. CareerPulse brings these signals into one analysis workspace.

Proposed solution

A data-driven web application with interactive analytics, an interpretable content-based job matcher, and a time-aware demand forecast. Results are calculated from the dataset imported by the user.

Technology stack

PythonFlaskPandasScikit-learnSQLitePlotlyBootstrap Icons

Methodology

Recommendations compare role and skill text with TF-IDF and only score structured preferences when the dataset includes those fields. Missing fields do not earn match points. In the supplied data, skills are inferred from titles rather than verified against descriptions. Demand forecasting aggregates dated postings monthly and uses lag features with a chronological holdout. Metrics are only shown when evaluation data is available.
